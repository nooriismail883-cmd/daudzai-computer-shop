Our Story

Noor Daudzai Computer Shop was founded with a simple mission: to make quality technology accessible to everyone. We started as a small computer repair shop and have grown into a trusted destination for refurbished computers and IT services.

We believe that everyone deserves access to reliable technology without breaking the bank. That's why we carefully select, test, and certify every product we sell, ensuring our customers get the best value for their money.

Our team of experienced technicians is passionate about technology and dedicated to providing exceptional customer service. Whether you need a laptop for work, a gaming PC, or computer repair services, we're here to help.
